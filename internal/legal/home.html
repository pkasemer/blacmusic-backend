Blac Music

Music and podcasts, with a focus on artists from Uganda

Blac Music is a streaming app where you can listen to music and podcasts, build playlists and follow your favourite artists, and where artists can publish their own music for anyone to hear.

For listeners

  • Stream tracks and albums, at a quality that suits your connection.
  • Save tracks, albums, artists and playlists to your library, and make your own playlists.
  • Subscribe to podcasts and pick up where you stopped.
  • Sign in with your email, Apple or Google.

For artists

  • Create an artist profile and upload your songs, albums and cover art. Schedule a release for the day you choose.
  • Optionally connect your YouTube channel so Blac Music can list your songs and prepare your releases for you. {{if .AudioFetch}}We read information about your videos using read-only access, and, if you ask for it, we import the audio of the songs you pick from your own channel; you can always upload your own files instead, and disconnect at any time.{{else}}We only read information about your videos, using read-only access; you upload the audio yourself, and you can disconnect at any time.{{end}}
  • You keep the rights to your music, and you can remove it whenever you like.

Your data

We use the information you give us, including what we receive from Apple, Google or YouTube when you choose to use them, only to run the service for you. We do not sell your data. Read the details in our Privacy Policy and the rules for using Blac Music in our Terms of Service.
