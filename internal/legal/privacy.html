Privacy Policy

Last updated {{.Updated}}

{{.Operator}} ("Blac Music", "we", "us") runs the Blac Music app and website at {{.SiteURL}}, a service for listening to music and podcasts and for artists to publish their music. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

Your account. Your email address, display name, and either a password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form) or the identifier of the Apple or Google account you signed in with. An optional profile picture. Whether your email address has been verified.

What you do in the app. Your playlists; the tracks, albums, artists and playlists you save or follow; podcast shows you subscribe to and where you stopped listening; your notification settings; and a record of the tracks you have played (which track, when, for how long and where in the app you started it), which we use to show artists how many plays and listeners their music has and to rank what is trending. When you start a mix, or autoplay carries on after a song, we also keep which song it started from, when, and which songs we suggested, to improve recommendations.

How you listen. To learn what you enjoy, the app also tells us how long you listened to a song and whether you finished, skipped or replayed it, where in the app you started it, when you like or unlike a song or add one to a playlist, and when your listening sessions start and end. We use this to recommend music and to keep your "Your sound" playlist up to date, which is a private playlist we change for you. It is not counted in artists' play numbers. Authorised members of our team can see a summary of your set-up choices and of what you have been listening to, such as the artists and genres you play, like and skip most. That summary does not show your searches or a song-by-song history.

What you choose when you set up the app. The languages you prefer and the languages you understand, regions, genres and artists you pick, and how widely you want to discover music. We keep these to recommend music to you. We do not treat what you leave out as a dislike, and we do not use these choices to work out your ethnicity.

If you are an artist. Your artist profile (name, bio), the audio files, cover art and details (titles, genres, release dates) you upload, your confirmation that you hold the rights to what you upload (with the date and the version of the terms), and how much storage you use.

Your device. If you allow notifications, a push notification token for your phone, its platform (iOS or Android) and the device name your phone reports.

Technical data. Our servers and security services record your IP address, the time and the request (for example when you sign in or stream a track). We use this to keep the service running, to prevent abuse such as repeated login attempts, and to investigate problems. Login attempts are rate-limited by IP address.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. Signing in with Apple or Google

If you choose "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google", the provider tells us a stable identifier for your account, your email address (Apple may give a private relay address) and your name. We use these only to create or find your Blac Music account. If the email address matches an existing account and the provider confirms it, we link the sign-in method to that account. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

3. Connecting your YouTube channel (optional, for artists)

Artists can connect their YouTube channel so we can find their songs and prepare their releases. This is optional and only starts when you press connect.

  • What we ask for: a single read-only permission (youtube.readonly). We can read your channel's name, ID and picture, and the titles, descriptions, publication dates, durations and thumbnails of your uploaded videos. We cannot change anything on your channel.

  • Importing the audio (optional): for songs you choose from your own connected channel, our server can fetch the audio-only stream of that video (not the picture) and store it as your upload, as if you had uploaded the file yourself. You must confirm that you own the rights to each song. We only do this for videos of the channel you connected, never for anyone else's. You can instead upload your own file for any song, and you can delete anything you imported.
    • {{else}}
  • What we do not do: we do not download, copy or store your videos or their audio from YouTube. Audio on Blac Music is always files you upload yourself.
    • {{end}}
  • What we do with it: we list your songs so you can choose which to turn into releases, pre-fill titles and release dates, and use a video's thumbnail as the release's cover art (you can replace it at any time).
  • How we protect it: the access token we keep is encrypted at rest and is never shown to anyone, including you.
  • How long we keep it: the video information we read is a cache. It is deleted when you disconnect, and we refresh or delete it within 30 days.
  • Disconnecting: disconnect in the app at any time. We then revoke our access with Google and delete the stored token and cached data. You can also remove Blac Music's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Blac Music's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the service: sign you in, play music and podcasts, keep your library and playlists, process and publish what artists upload.
  • To send you messages you asked for or that concern your account: an email verification code, and push notifications (for example that an import has finished or a scheduled release is live). You can switch notification types off in the app.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To follow the law and to enforce our Terms of Service, including handling copyright complaints.

5. What is public

Artist profiles, published releases and tracks, and the name and picture shown on them are public. A playlist you mark public, together with your display name and picture as its owner, can be seen and saved by others. Your library, private playlists, listening position, email address and notification settings are not public. Artists see how many plays and listeners their music has (numbers per day, track and release); they are not told who you are.

6. Who we share data with

We use service providers to run Blac Music. They process data on our behalf and only for that purpose:

  • our hosting provider, which runs the servers and storage;
  • Cloudflare, which sits in front of our website and API (it sees requests, including IP addresses);
  • Expo, Apple and Google, which deliver push notifications (they receive your device token and the notification text);
  • Apple and Google, if you use their sign-in or connect YouTube;
  • Plunk (useplunk.com), which sends our emails, such as your verification code (it receives your email address and the message).

Some of these providers are outside Uganda, so your data may be processed in other countries. We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect people from harm. We do not sell or rent personal data.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings). Deleting it removes your account, sign-in methods, sessions, playlists, library, mix and autoplay history, set-up choices and the recommendation profile started from them, how you listened, subscriptions, notification tokens, YouTube connection and profile pictures (apart from a picture that stays with an artist profile, as explained below). Your play history stays only as anonymous numbers in artists' statistics (it is no longer linked to you). Queued notifications are deleted after 14 days at the latest, and technical logs are kept for a limited period. Data in backups, if any, is removed when those backups expire.

If you are an artist, what happens to your music depends on how you got your artist profile. Whenever tracks are deleted, they also disappear from other people's playlists and libraries.

  • A profile you created. Deleting your account also deletes the profile, with all its releases, tracks and uploaded files, including any our team added to it.
  • A profile we transferred to you from our catalogue. Our team can hand you an artist profile that is already on Blac Music, for example when you sign up. If you delete your account, that profile goes back to our catalogue. We delete the songs and audio files you uploaded yourself, any release that held only your uploads, your unfinished uploads and your profile picture. The profile itself, the songs our team added and the other releases stay, together with their details, including any changes you made to them, such as the name, biography, descriptions, lyrics, credits and cover pictures.
  • A profile we removed from your account. Our team can also take an artist profile away from an account, for example if it went to the wrong person. Your account stays, but you can no longer manage that profile. Its releases and tracks stay as they are, including songs and cover pictures you uploaded, and published ones stay public. The profile keeps the picture it was showing at that moment, which may be your own profile picture. Uploads you had not finished sending are deleted; files that were already waiting to be processed still become songs. Deleting your account later does not delete what stayed with that profile.

Asking us to remove what stayed. If something you uploaded or wrote stays with an artist profile you no longer manage, you can ask us to delete it{{if .Email}} by writing to {{.Email}}{{end}}, even after you have deleted your account. We will check that the request comes from you and then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Our team's activity record. We keep a record of what our team does to accounts, artist profiles and content, such as a suspension, a deletion or a transfer, with who did it, when, why and the email address of the account concerned. We use it for security and accountability. It cannot be edited, and it is kept after an account is deleted.

8. Security

Connections to Blac Music are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and connection tokens for third parties are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us if you think your account has been misused.

9. Your rights

You can see and correct most of your data in the app, and delete your account in Settings. You may also ask us to give you a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to withdraw a consent you gave, subject to the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda and other laws that apply to you. Write to us using the contact details below. You also have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Office in Uganda.

10. Children

Blac Music is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, please use the service with the permission of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
